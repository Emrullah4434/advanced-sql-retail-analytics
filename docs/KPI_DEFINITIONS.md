# KPI definitions

- **Net sales**: SUM(quantity × unit_price × (1 − discount_rate)) for `Completed` orders. This is pre-tax; no returns or shipping costs are modeled.
- **MoM revenue growth**: (current month revenue / prior month revenue − 1) × 100.
- **Category share**: category net sales / all category net sales × 100.
- **Customer lifetime value (simplified)**: historical completed-order sales value per customer; not predictive CLV.
- **Repeat purchaser**: a customer with a second or later completed order at the month of measurement.
